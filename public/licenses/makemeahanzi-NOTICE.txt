Character component data (char_parts.json)
==========================================

Learn Hanzi's character breakdowns (the parts and etymology notes shown
when learning a new character, and the look-alike answers in quizzes) come
from Make Me a Hanzi by Shaunak Kishore:

    https://github.com/skishore/makemeahanzi

Source file: dictionary.txt at commit
618dbab8a8ddefb958763c8b4afbaa741a4460de
    https://github.com/skishore/makemeahanzi/blob/618dbab8a8ddefb958763c8b4afbaa741a4460de/dictionary.txt

Modifications: char_parts.json is a modified subset of dictionary.txt. It
keeps only the characters used in Learn Hanzi's vocabulary and the
components they are built from, keeps the decomposition, etymology, first
pinyin reading and a shortened first definition, and renames fields to
short keys. The script that produces it, build-char-parts.py, is in the
Learn Hanzi repository; you can rebuild or modify char_parts.json from the
original dictionary.txt with it.
